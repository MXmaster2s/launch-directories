# Listing plan: longbook (an example)

A finished plan, kept as an example of the format in `TEMPLATE.md`.

## Brief

- **Product:** longbook (one word, all lowercase)
- **What it is:** a memory for your financial commitments on iPhone and Mac, which your
  AI can read through an MCP server
- **Site:** https://longbook.app
- **Takes:** ios, mac, app, mcp, claude-plugin, chatgpt-plugin, maker
- **Released:** yes, on the App Store
- **Ready to use:** short and long descriptions, logo, screenshots, a reviewer login for
  the AI app stores
